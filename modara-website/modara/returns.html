Returns & Warranty

We want you to feel confident buying from MODARA. Here's exactly how returns and warranty coverage work.

Return Window

You may request a return within 7 days of delivery, provided the watch is unworn, in its original packaging, with all tags and documentation intact.

How to Start a Return

  • Contact our support team via the Contact page with your order number.
  • We'll confirm eligibility and send you a return authorization and shipping instructions.
  • Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund or exchange is processed within 5–7 business days.

Warranty Coverage

Every MODARA watch includes a manufacturer-backed warranty (1–2 years, listed on each product page) covering defects in movement and materials under normal use. This does not cover water damage beyond the stated resistance rating, accidental damage, or normal wear such as scratches on the case or strap.

Non-Returnable Items

Gift sets that have been opened and used, and any item marked "Final Sale" at checkout, are not eligible for return.

Questions

Reach out any time via our Contact page and we'll walk you through the process.
