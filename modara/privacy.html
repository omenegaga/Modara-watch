Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. This policy explains what information MODARA collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you shop with us, we collect information you provide directly — name, email, phone number, delivery address, and payment details processed securely through our payment partners. We also collect basic usage data such as pages visited and device type to improve the site.

How We Use It

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To communicate order updates, and — with your consent — new arrivals and offers
  • To improve site performance and product selection
  • To prevent fraud and protect our customers

Payment Information

MODARA does not store your full card details. Payments are processed through PCI-compliant partners including Paystack and Flutterwave.

Sharing

We share information only with the delivery, payment, and analytics partners needed to run the store — never sold to third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time, and can request access to or deletion of your data by contacting support@modara.ng.
