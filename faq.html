Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before, during and after your order.

Shipping & Delivery
Orders to Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt typically arrive in 2–4 business days. Other locations across Nigeria take 3–6 business days. Delivery estimates are also shown on each product page.
Once your order ships, you'll receive an email and SMS with a tracking link. You can also check order status anytime from your Account page.
We currently ship nationwide within Nigeria. International shipping is in development — join the MODARA Club to be notified when it launches.
Payments
We accept major debit and credit cards, bank transfers, and USSD payments, processed securely through Paystack and Flutterwave.
Yes. Checkout is encrypted end-to-end and MODARA never stores your full card details on our servers.
Warranty & Authenticity
Yes. Every watch is sourced through verified suppliers and inspected before listing. Authenticity is a non-negotiable part of our quality standard.
Most watches carry a 1–2 year MODARA warranty covering manufacturing defects in movement and materials. Coverage length is listed on each product page.
Returns
Yes — see our Returns & Warranty page for the full policy, timelines and step-by-step process.
